Frequently Asked Questions

Everything You Need To Know

Common questions about selling on Cubrin — answered.

What does it cost to sell on Cubrin?
Onboarding is completely free. Cubrin only charges a flat 2% transaction fee when you make a sale. No monthly subscriptions, no hidden charges.

Get your free branded banner →
Do I need a POS machine to sell on Cubrin?
No POS hardware is needed. You run your entire digital store from your smartphone. Cubrin provides a dynamic virtual NUBAN for instant settlements.
What kind of vendors can onboard Cubrin?
Cubrin accepts food vendors (buka, grills, snacks, fast food, provisions), beauty and personal care vendors, device and gadget sellers, and fashion and home lifestyle vendors.
How do I get paid on Cubrin?
Payments are escrow-backed. Customer funds are locked before the order is sent to you. Once you fulfill the order, funds are released automatically to your verified bank account.
Can I handle delivery myself on Cubrin?
Yes. Vendors on Cubrin handle delivery their own way — no need to hand items to unknown agents. You control the entire fulfilment process.
