Everything you need to know about the Cubrin ecosystem.

What exactly is Cubrin?
Cubrin is a robust social commerce and hyperlocal delivery platform designed to connect local communities, students, and merchants. We make it seamless to find local food, coordinate group payments, and get items delivered instantly.
How does the "Split Bill" feature work?
Ordering with roommates or friends? Instead of one person paying and hunting down everyone else for cash, you can split the cost of your cart directly inside the app before the order is placed. Everyone pays their share from their Cubrin wallet.
What is the "Pay for Me" feature?
If you are out of funds or want someone else to treat you, you can build your cart and send a "Pay for Me" request directly to a friend or partner's app. Once they approve and pay, your local delivery is processed immediately.
Is my money safe if my order isn't delivered?
Absolutely. Cubrin uses an Escrow-Backed Delivery system. When you pay, your funds are held securely in a dedicated ledger. The merchant only receives the funds once the delivery is verified by both you and the merchant, eliminating any risk.
Can I use Cubrin to see food prices before I travel?
Yes! Our Hyperlocal Price Radar acts as a cost-of-living map. If you are moving to a new campus or neighborhood, you can search that specific area to instantly view the average cost of meals and locate the most affordable spots before you arrive.
Can I book group outings or send gifts?
Yes. Cubrin features an integrated booking engine that allows you to plan dates, coordinate group schedules, and send localized meals or gifts directly to loved ones.
What is a Cubrin Ambassador (Connector)?
Ambassadors are the driving force of our decentralized network. They act as local scouts who onboard neighborhood merchants, Bukas, and fast-food spots onto the platform using our proprietary Ambassador Portal.
How do I earn money as an Ambassador?
You earn passive, recurring income. Ambassadors receive up to 10% of the platform fees generated by every single merchant they successfully onboard, creating a sustainable commission stream.
How do I register local merchants?
Once accepted into the program, you will gain access to the secure Cubrin Management Panel. From there, you can easily register local spots without them needing to buy expensive POS hardware or go through complicated setups.
When and how do I get paid?
Commissions are routed through our automated settlement API. Payouts are sent programmatically to your registered bank account or Cubrin wallet based on our standard tier-payout structures.
Do I have to pay to join the Ambassador program?
No. Joining the program is entirely free. You simply need to apply through our waitlist. We review and approve Ambassadors based on campus density and location.
How does the "Eat Now, Pay Later" (ENPL) feature work?
Partnering with Cubrin provides lending companies zero-CAC access to a high-volume, verified youth demographic. Since loans are for low-value food transactions and funds never leave our closed-loop escrow ecosystem, partners can safely underwrite micro-credit risk.
As a local merchant, why should I sell on Cubrin?
Cubrin gives you immediate access to digital infrastructure without the overhead. You receive a dynamic virtual NUBAN for settlements, a digital storefront, and access to a highly engaged community of students.
Are there hidden fees for merchants?
No. Cubrin charges a flat percentage applied strictly to transactions. Our automated split-payment APIs seamlessly route platform fees and taxes (VAT), so what hits your settlement account is purely yours.
How does the Escrow ledger protect my business?
Our escrow system guarantees that the customer has already paid before you start preparing the order. You never have to worry about unpaid fulfillments, fake transfers, or out-of-pocket losses.
