About Cubrin

1. Overview

Cubrin is a technology platform that connects users with nearby independent vendors such as food vendors, restaurants, grills, snack providers, and other local merchants.

Through the Cubrin mobile application, users can discover nearby places, place orders, coordinate payments, and interact with vendors and friends in a structured, commerce-focused environment.

Cubrin's primary purpose is to make local commerce more accessible, organized, and convenient.

2. What Cubrin Does

Cubrin provides a digital marketplace and infrastructure that enables:

  • discovery of nearby vendors based on user location,
  • browsing of vendor menus and items,
  • placing and tracking orders,
  • in-app payments using a closed-loop platform balance,
  • split payments and shared bill coordination,
  • "pay-for-me" and peer-assisted payment requests,
  • vendor communication related to orders,
  • reviews and feedback on vendors and services.

Cubrin acts as a facilitator between users and independent vendors.

3. What Cubrin Is Not

To avoid misunderstanding, Cubrin is not:

  • a bank or financial institution,
  • a money transfer service,
  • a credit or lending provider,
  • a food production or delivery company,
  • or a direct seller of goods or services.

Cubrin does not own or operate the vendors listed on the platform and does not control their pricing, inventory, or fulfillment operations.

4. Independent Vendors

All vendors on Cubrin operate as independent businesses. They are solely responsible for:

  • product quality and availability,
  • pricing accuracy,
  • order fulfillment,
  • business operations,
  • and compliance with applicable laws and regulations.

Cubrin provides the tools for vendors to connect with customers but does not control their day-to-day operations.

5. Payments and Platform Balance

Cubrin may provide users with a limited-purpose platform balance used exclusively for transactions within the Cubrin ecosystem. This balance is used only for:

  • purchasing goods and services within Cubrin,
  • completing split payments,
  • and facilitating platform transactions.

It is not a bank account, is not interest-bearing, and cannot be used outside the Cubrin platform. Payments are processed using third-party payment providers, and Cubrin does not directly operate as a financial institution.

6. Communication and Social Features

Cubrin includes structured communication tools that allow users to interact in relation to:

  • orders,
  • payments,
  • shared bills,
  • and approved connections between users.

Messaging is permission-based and not an open public chat system.

7. User Experience

Users can:

  • discover nearby vendors,
  • place and track orders,
  • interact with friends for shared payments,
  • submit reviews and feedback,
  • and manage their platform activity and balance.

All actions within Cubrin are designed to support local commerce interactions.

8. Platform Role

Cubrin is a neutral technology platform that facilitates connections and transactions between users and independent vendors. Cubrin does not:

  • guarantee vendor performance,
  • control vendor pricing or inventory,
  • or assume responsibility for goods or services provided by vendors.

9. Relationship to Other Policies

This document provides a general overview of Cubrin. Detailed rules governing usage, payments, privacy, vendor responsibilities, disputes, and refunds are outlined in the respective legal policies available on the platform. In case of conflict, the specific policy documents shall take precedence over this overview.

10. Updates

Cubrin may update this document from time to time to reflect product, legal, or operational changes. Continued use of the platform indicates acceptance of updates.

Refund & Escrow Policy

1. Purpose

This Policy explains the conditions under which refunds are issued, how escrow is handled, and how transaction reversals may occur on the Cubrin platform. Cubrin facilitates transactions between users and independent vendors and uses escrow mechanisms to support secure order fulfillment.

2. Escrow Mechanism

Cubrin may temporarily hold funds in escrow when a user places an order. Escrow funds are held to: ensure order confirmation, support successful fulfillment by vendors, protect against failed or disputed transactions, and enable platform integrity. Funds are released from escrow only when: an order is confirmed as completed, or a valid resolution is reached in case of dispute. Escrow does not represent banking, savings, or custodial services.

3. Refund Eligibility

3.1 Failed Orders: If a vendor fails to fulfill an order due to unavailability or operational issues.
3.2 Order Cancellation: If an order is cancelled within the allowed cancellation window before preparation or fulfillment begins.
3.3 Incorrect or Incomplete Orders: If the delivered item significantly differs from the order placed or is incomplete.
3.4 Duplicate Transactions: If a user is charged multiple times for the same order due to technical or processing errors.
3.5 Platform or System Errors: If a transaction fails or is incorrectly processed due to a system malfunction.

4. Non-Refundable Situations

Refunds may not be issued in cases where: the order has been successfully completed and confirmed, the user changes their mind after vendor preparation has begun, incorrect user-provided information leads to failed delivery, or disputes arise outside of platform-controlled processes. Cubrin may evaluate such cases at its discretion.

5. Cancellation Policy

Users may cancel orders within a limited timeframe before the vendor confirms or begins preparation. Once an order is confirmed or preparation has started: cancellation may no longer be possible, or may be subject to partial refund at Cubrin's discretion. Vendors may also cancel orders due to inability to fulfill, in which case refunds will be processed where applicable.

6. Delayed or Unfulfilled Orders

If a vendor fails to confirm or fulfill an order within the expected timeframe: the order may be automatically flagged as delayed, Cubrin may intervene or cancel the order, and eligible refunds may be issued.

7. Duplicate or Erroneous Transactions

If duplicate payments occur due to technical errors: Cubrin will investigate the transaction logs, and issue refunds or reversals where confirmed. Users are advised to report such issues through official support channels.

8. Suspicious or Fraudulent Activity

Cubrin reserves the right to: pause or reverse transactions suspected to be fraudulent, temporarily hold funds during investigation, restrict account access where abuse is detected, and cooperate with third-party payment providers and law enforcement where required.

9. Refund Method

Where approved, refunds may be issued as: Cubrin Balance credits, or reversal to the original payment method via third-party processors. Refund processing times may vary depending on payment provider timelines.

10. Platform Role Disclaimer

Cubrin acts solely as a technology platform facilitating transactions between users and vendors. Cubrin does not guarantee vendor performance and is not responsible for goods or services provided by vendors beyond platform-defined refund conditions.

11. Policy Updates

Cubrin may update this Policy at any time. Continued use of the platform constitutes acceptance of any changes.
