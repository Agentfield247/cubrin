The Cubrin Balance is a limited-purpose transactional balance used exclusively for commerce within Cubrin. It is not a bank account, savings product, or deposit account. No interest is earned. Cubrin may place holds, reverse transactions, or suspend balances for fraud prevention.

Privacy Policy

Information we collect: Account info, precise location (foreground/background), usage data, messages, payment transaction data (no full card details stored). For vendors: KYC documents, bank details.

Data Sharing: We share order-related data with vendors, payment processors, delivery partners, and legal authorities if required. We do not sell personal data.

Payment & Balance Policy

The Cubrin Balance is closed-loop, non-transferable, not redeemable as cash. Refunds may be issued to balance or original payment method. Chargeback abuse may lead to account restrictions.

Refund & Escrow Policy
